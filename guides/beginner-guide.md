## What game are you starting?

Permafrost entered Steam Early Access on October 9, 2026. It is still in development. You play as Rook, an engineer drawn into a frozen world by a radio message from an old friend. Solo play and online co-op for up to four players are confirmed.

The publisher describes exploration, scavenging, hunting, crafting, and restoring shelters as core activities. Progression involves recovering shelters and technology while exploring.

## Before your first trip — editorial checklist

- Read the active objective and on-screen interaction prompts before following an older walkthrough.
- Check the current controls in your settings, especially interaction, inventory, movement, and building.
- Observe your status indicators after moving between exposed areas and shelter; respond to the warnings shown by your game.
- Inspect a recipe in the crafting interface before collecting a large batch of materials.
- Plan a return route to a known shelter. In co-op, agree on when to regroup.

These are planning suggestions, not a tested sequence or a promise that particular milestones take a fixed number of minutes.
