Welcome to the frozen apocalypse of ***Permafrost***, developed by SpaceRocket Games and published by Toplitz Productions. Following "The Shattering" — the catastrophic lunar destruction that plunged Earth into a perpetual sub-zero winter — survival demands calculated resource prioritization and unwavering thermal awareness.

As the protagonist **Rook**, you wake up with limited supplies in a merciless white desert. This guide outlines the verified step-by-step roadmap to survive your critical first 60 minutes.

---

## 1. Understanding the 5 Core Survival Stats

Before venturing away from your spawn point, monitor your health indicators located on the bottom HUD:

| Stat Name | Depletion Condition | Consequences of Reaching Zero | Recovery Methods |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Body Warmth** | Ambient cold exceeds clothing insulation | Causes **Hypothermia**; rapid continuous HP loss | Campfire, indoor shelter, Makeshift Heater |
| **Health (HP)** | Hypothermia, predator attacks, fall damage | Permadeath / Inventory drop at grave marker | Makeshift Bandage, Cooked Meat, Herbal Tea |
| **Hunger** | Continuous exertion and passage of time | Max stamina reduction; starvation damage | Cooked Meat (Venison/Rabbit), Canned Rations |
| **Energy** | Staying awake over prolonged cycles | Reduced movement speed and vision blur | Sleeping on a bed roll or crafted cot |
| **Stamina** | Sprinting, heavy melee swings, climbing | Inability to dodge or run from hostile wolves | Regenerates automatically when resting |

> **Key Rule:** Early Access does **not** feature farmable agriculture. You cannot grow crops. Food must come from hunting wildlife or looting abandoned industrial ruins.

## 2. Opening 15 Minutes: Starter Tool Progression

Follow this strict progression sequence upon waking up:

### Step 1: Collect Surface Loose Branches & Rocks
Do not punch trees barehanded. Search the snowy ground for loose **Branches** and **Loose Stones**. You need:
* 4x Wood
* 2x Small Stones

### Step 2: Craft Your First Stone Axe
Open your inventory crafting menu (`Tab` or `I`):
* **Stone Axe Recipe:** 4x Wood + 2x Small Stone
* Equip to your hotbar (`Slot 1`). Use this axe to chop down non-frozen birch and pine trees for substantial timber. Avoid fully ice-encased tree trunks, as they cannot be harvested.

### Step 3: Craft the Stone Pickaxe
* **Stone Pickaxe Recipe:** 4x Wood + 2x Small Stone
* Use the pickaxe to break surface boulders to obtain **Flint** and additional stones. Flint is the gateway to Tier 2 mining tools.

## 3. Minutes 15–30: Establishing Emergency Thermal Shelter

Nightfall in Permafrost is lethal without preparations. Temperatures plunge below -30°C, draining starter clothing warmth in seconds.

1. **Locate an Intact Building Shell:** Search for a ruined wooden cabin or concrete warehouse with intact ceiling rafters.
2. **Build a Campfire:**
   * Craft with **2x Stone + 4x Wood**.
   * Place inside your sheltered perimeter out of blizzard wind tunnels.
   * Add wood or charcoal to ignite. Stand within 2 meters to replenish your **Body Warmth** to 100%.
3. **Melt Clean Snow for Drinking Water:**
   * Scoop fresh snow with an empty container.
   * Boil over campfire embers to eliminate biological contaminants. Never consume dirty raw snow, or you will suffer hypothermic stomach cramps.

---

## 4. Minutes 30–45: The Crude Bow & Food Security

Relying on canned beans will deplete your supply within hours. Secure permanent protein through bow hunting:

* **Crafting Rope:** Gather 2x Cloth from ruined furniture and clothing racks -> Craft **Rope** (Hand-crafted).
* **Crafting Crude Bow:** 2x Wood + 1x Rope.
* **Crafting Stone Arrows:** 1x Wood + 1x Small Stone yields 3 arrows.
* **Hunting Strategy:** Crouch (`C` key) to mask footstep noise. Target wild deer and snow hares from 20 meters away. Always harvest their hides for insulation crafting.
* **Cooking:** Place Raw Meat on the campfire grill. Cook until golden brown. Eating raw meat risks severe parasite infection.

---

## 5. Minutes 45–60: Next Milestones

Once your warmth and hunger are stabilized:
1. **Recruit Your Dog Companion:** Northeast of your starting valley lies the **Bone Cave**. Prepare 1x Bandage and 1x Raw Meat to recruit your four-legged partner.
2. **Push to Horizon Settlement:** Follow the radio beacon towards Horizon to meet allied traders and unlock high-tier workbench schematics.
3. **Begin Smelting:** Build a Stone Furnace to smelt Copper Ore mined with your Flint Pickaxe.
