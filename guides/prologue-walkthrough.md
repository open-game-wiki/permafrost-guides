## Applicable version
Reviewed **2026-10-15**. This route summarizes Saad Bin Asif's [October 12 prologue walkthrough](https://intoindiegames.com/walkthroughs/permafrost-prologue-walkthrough/) for PC Early Access. No build number is supplied. It is a media-reported route, not an independently reproduced test.

## Opening checkpoints — media report
1. Gather wood and loose stones; place a campfire and add wood as fuel.
2. Craft stone tools, then use a shiv to enter the cabin. Cloth leads into rope and bow progression.
3. Reach Horizon and speak with Robyn. The route then includes cooking and the injured dog in Bone Cave.
4. Establish shelter, obtain flint and a workbench, then unlock the Stone Furnace.
5. Progress through copper ingots, wire and the Radio Station Bench; later repair work uses scrap from a dismantling bench.

## Avoid copying quantities blindly
The source gives collection totals, but these should not be treated as per-item recipes. Its copper illustration caption also refers to iron. This page therefore supplies neither a mining coordinate nor a recipe table from that passage. Inspect your current crafting panel before gathering materials.

## Co-op caveat — developer confirmation
The [developer's known-issues post](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2254990/discussions/0/588440207635776246/), updated October 14, says guests can join after the host reaches the cabin, the world has one dog belonging to the host, and the host should perform main story and Skinwalker steps. These are current workaround notes, not permanent design guarantees.
