## Is Permafrost coming to PS5?
A PlayStation Store concept page exists for Permafrost from Toplitz Productions and identifies a PS5 version. On October 28, 2026 it was marked as announced, with the release date still to come. This establishes an announced listing, not a playable PS5 release today.

## Is October 9 the PS5 release date?
No. October 9, 2026 is the Steam Early Access release date. It does not supply a date for the PlayStation listing. Check the platform's own store before planning a console session.

## What about Xbox?
The sources checked for this article do not establish an Xbox release date or current availability. That is a limit of the confirmed information, not proof that an Xbox version can never happen. Follow publisher announcements for a dated platform update.

## Does co-op mean cross-platform play?
No. PC online co-op and an announced console listing do not establish PC-to-console crossplay or shared saves. Do not assume a Steam purchase grants a console license.
