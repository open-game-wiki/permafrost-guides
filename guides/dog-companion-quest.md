Surviving the arctic apocalypse of *Permafrost* is a lonely ordeal, but SpaceRocket Games made the dog companion one of the most mechanically rewarding features of the game. Beyond companionship, your canine partner is a living radar, pack mule, and hunting assistant.

Here is the complete walkthrough on how to recruit your dog during the **"Dog Days"** quest.

---

## 1. Prerequisites: What to Pack Before You Leave

Do **not** visit the Bone Cave unprepared. If you reach the dog without the required supplies, you cannot complete the recruitment interaction and will risk freezing on a fruitless return trip:

1. **1x Makeshift Bandage:** Craft in your hand inventory with **2x Cloth**. (The dog is injured and bleeding from an encounter with mutated predators).
2. **1x Raw Meat:** Harvest from a killed deer or rabbit using your Stone Knife or Crude Bow. (Do **not** cook this piece of meat; the dog requires raw sustenance to establish predatory trust).
3. **Warm Clothes + 6x Wood:** Ensure your body warmth will survive a 10-minute round trip.

---

## 2. Navigating to Bone Cave

* **Location:** Northeast of your starting valley and starter shelter.
* **Landmarks:** Look for a prominent jagged limestone ridge crowned by dead pine trees.
* **Coordinates / Compass:** Follow the compass needle approximately northeast (bearing 045°). As you approach within 80 meters, Rook will comment on whimpering sounds coming from a rock cleft.
* **Cave Entrance:** A narrow fissure partially blocked by ice stalagmites. Break them with your pickaxe or squeeze through crouching.

---

## 3. The "Dog Days" Recruitment Sequence

1. **Approach Slowly:** Enter the cave interior. The injured dog will growl defensively from behind a boulder.
2. **First Interaction (Bandage):** Walk within prompt range. Press `E` to initiate treatment. Rook will wrap the dog's bleeding leg with the **Makeshift Bandage**.
3. **Second Interaction (Feeding):** Press `E` again to offer the **Raw Meat**. The dog will consume the meal, whimper gratefully, and stand up on four legs.
4. **Naming / Bonding:** A prompt allows you to officially register your companion. The "Dog Days" quest completes, rewarding **150 Survival XP** and the **Dog Whistle**.

---

## 4. Canine Mechanics & In-Game Abilities

### A. Threat Detection (Early Warning Radar)
When hostile wolves, aggressive bears, or armed Moon Clan scouts approach within **30 meters**, your dog assumes a defensive stance, points its snout toward the threat, and emits a low guttural growl. This gives you ample time to draw your bow or seek high ground.

### B. Scent Tracking (`G` Key)
Holding `G` opens the command wheel. Selecting **"Track Scent"** prompts your dog to sniff the snow. It will guide you directly to:
* Blood trails of wounded animals you shot with an arrow;
* Hidden supply crates buried beneath snowdrifts;
* Abandoned survival caches.

### C. Pack Saddle (Carrying Capacity Expansion)
Craft a **Dog Saddle Bag** at the Crafting Bench:
* **Recipe:** 4x Treated Leather + 2x Metal Buckles + 2x Rope.
* Equipping the saddle adds **+8 dedicated inventory slots** and increases the dog's carry weight by 25 kg, effectively solving early-game inventory crunches during resource runs.
