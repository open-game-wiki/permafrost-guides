## Why the Dog Companion is Crucial

In the frozen wasteland of *Permafrost*, your canine companion is not just a cosmetic pet—it serves three critical survival functions:
1. **Threat Scouting & Early Warning**: Growls and barks when predators (wolves, bears) or hostile bandits approach before they spot you.
2. **Mobile Inventory Hauler**: Equipping dog packs provides additional inventory slots to carry raw materials and loot during scavenging runs.
3. **Tracking & Exploration**: Alerts you to hidden resource caches buried beneath the snowdrifts.

---

## Step 1: Complete Early Prerequisites

The dog companion quest does not activate immediately upon starting the game. You must complete the early tutorial milestones first:
1. Finish the opening prologue objectives: *A New Beginning* and *Feel The Heat*.
2. Reach the **Horizon Settlement** (the first major human outpost).
3. Speak with **Robyn** inside the leader's cabin and complete the quest *Horizon's Kitchen* (cook a piece of raw meat at the settlement's stone hearth).

---

## Step 2: Accept the "Dog Days" Quest

Once *Horizon's Kitchen* is turned in, talk to Robyn again. She will give you the primary companion quest: **"Dog Days"**, pointing you toward the **Bone Cave** north of the settlement.

### Required Expedition Supplies (Prepare Before Leaving):
* **Light Source**: Equipped via the quick radial menu (`Q` key by default) or craft a simple torch.
* **1x Makeshift Bandage**: Crafted from 2x Cloth. Used to heal the dog's injured leg.
* **1x Raw Meat**: Harvested from hunted game or scavenged from coolers.
* **Weapon**: A Crafted Stone Axe or Crude Bow (2x Wood, 1x Rope) to deal with cave wildlife.

---

## Step 3: Clear Bone Cave & Rescue the Pup

1. Follow the compass marker north through the frozen valley toward **Bone Cave**.
2. **Caution**: A hostile predator (bear or timber wolf depending on difficulty) guards the interior entrance. Maintain distance with a bow or use sprint-dodge attacks.
3. Approach the injured whimpering dog trapped at the back of the cavern.
4. Interact (`F` key) to apply the **Makeshift Bandage**, then offer the **Raw Meat**.
5. Once fed and healed, the dog will stand up and bond with your character.

---

## Step 4: Companion Commands & Upgrades

* **Follow / Stay**: Aim at the companion and use the interaction wheel to toggle between following you or guarding a specific shelter radius.
* **Saddlebags & Storage**: Craft a Leather Dog Harness at the Tailoring Bench (unlocked via Quest progression) to expand its carrying capacity.
* **Cold & Health Management**: Ensure you build an insulated Dog Bed near your hearth so your companion recovers stamina during blizzards.
