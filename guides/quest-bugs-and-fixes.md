## Read the status before trying a workaround
Checked October 28, 2026. **Released** means the developer announced a fix, not that every old save has been tested here. **Testing** is not a released fix. The pinned October 16 workarounds are historical and may be superseded by later patches.

The latest checked announcement only displayed a relative time, so this table identifies it by title rather than guessing a publication date.

## Extreme Makeover: no repair spots or 0/5
The October 16 pinned post describes missing repair spots at the Rat Queen camp. For players who **already renovated it**, it suggests checking Logan's dialogue for an option that the work is already taken care of. That is a conditional historical workaround, not a universal repair or a new set of five confirmed locations.

If the spots are absent and no appropriate dialogue exists, retain the save and report that state. Do not demolish and rebuild the camp, edit quest data or reset a world based on an unverified claim.

## Hopes and Dreams: Listen To Recording
The pinned post says playing the recording from the journal can complete that objective. It separately describes early pickup and out-of-order problems; these should not be combined into one guaranteed fix. Check your build and the exact objective before repeating a pickup route. The later The Lost Wife fix does not prove all recording-related tasks are interchangeable.

## Keys and co-op: use the date with the advice
The October 16 post suggests asking Anna or Skinwalker again for certain missing quest keys. It also contains host-only and dialogue-skip workarounds for older builds. Since October 21 addresses several of those quests, first compare the current patch status and objective. Do not assume the old host workaround is still mandatory for all multiplayer tasks.

## Report a remaining block
1. Keep a separate copy of your existing save before further experiments; do not overwrite your only copy.
2. Note the build, exact quest/objective, when items were collected, and whether the world is solo or co-op.
3. For co-op, identify who accepted, interacted and received the reward.
4. Attach screenshots and reproduction steps through the developer's [support upload](https://filehub.toplitz-productions.com/) if requested. Review files before sharing.
