## Version and source review
Checked **2026-10-15**. Scope: PC Early Access, with developer workarounds updated **2026-10-14**. Neither source below supplies a current retail build number. Preview build **0.44.2** data mentioned by the fan guide is not treated as current retail recipe evidence.

## Blueprint and bench progression — secondary source
The [October 14 fan crafting guide](https://www.permafrost-wiki.site/guides/crafting-guide) distinguishes level-earned blueprint points, world recipes and quest recipes. It describes a workbench followed by furnace progression, with recipes tied to their stations. This is secondary reporting, not our test.

Editorial checklist: inspect the locked recipe, its unlock requirement and the workstation named in your current interface. Spend points on the next usable step toward your active objective. Do not assume a blueprint drop always means the item is implemented.

## Moon Rock tool correction — developer confirmation
The [developer known-issues post](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2254990/discussions/0/588440207635776246/) says **Moon Rock requires the quality iron pickaxe**. The basic iron pickaxe tooltip is misleading. The same post says controller crafting is not working yet, and a refined flint sledgehammer blueprint can drop even though that item is not yet implemented.

If mining or crafting fails, check the exact tool name and input method before collecting more resources. Recheck the developer post after a patch; these notes may become obsolete.

## Why there is no exact recipe table
The previous table lacked traceable build-specific evidence and has been removed. We cannot label those costs or workstation unlock quests as community-tested. Use the live recipe panel for quantities; collection targets from a walkthrough are not smelting ratios.
