## Looking for the Permafrost game map?
This page is a route-planning guide, not an interactive map or a verified coordinate database. Start with the map and objective information available in your installed build. The [expedition planner](/tools/expedition-planner/) lets you keep your own return point and supply checklist.

## Plan an outward route and a return
1. Write down the active objective, the destination shown in your game and your last usable shelter.
2. Pick one objective for this trip. Note a recognizable junction, building or terrain feature before leaving a safe route.
3. Check the supplies and equipment required by your current task. Use the actual crafting screen for recipe quantities.
4. Decide what would make you return: a warning shown by your game, missing equipment, completed work or insufficient carrying room.
5. After returning, record the route, relevant landmarks and game build. Separate places you visited from places mentioned by another player.

These are editorial planning steps. They do not establish travel times, safe temperatures or guaranteed resource respawns.

## Fast travel: check old screenshots against the October 15 patch
The developer's **Gamepad, Fast Travel & Quest Fixes** update says the fast-travel points near the Warehouse and Crow's Nest were moved to avoid collisions, and missing fast-travel interaction text was fixed. If an older screenshot points to an obstructed position, inspect the nearby area and compare the patch installed in your game. This note does not establish new coordinates or an unlock order.

Source: [developer update in the Permafrost community hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2254990), October 15, 2026; checked October 28.

## When a marker or route does not match
Check whether the objective has prerequisites, whether you are following advice for an earlier patch and whether the source records a visited location or only a planned destination. Compare the current [developer announcements](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2254990/announcements/) before abandoning a save because an old screenshot differs.

For a persistent issue, note the objective text, installed build, landmark and reproduction steps. The publisher's [feedback board](https://permafrost.featureupvote.com/) explains how to report bugs and directs technical support requests to its support service.
