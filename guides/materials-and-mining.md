Progress in *Permafrost* is strictly gated by your tool tiers. Attempting to strike a Copper vein with a primitive Stone Pickaxe will yield zero ore while destroying your tool's durability.

To conquer the frozen wilderness, you must understand the tool tier hierarchy and know where high-value veins spawn across the map.

---

## 1. The 5-Tier Pickaxe Hierarchy

| Tool Tier | Required Tool | Materials Harvested | Tool Crafting Ingredients |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Tier 0** | Bare Hands | Loose Wood, Loose Rocks, Surface Snow | Free |
| **Tier 1** | **Stone Pickaxe** | Surface Rock, **Flint Nodes**, Basic Clay | 4x Wood, 2x Small Stone |
| **Tier 2** | **Flint Pickaxe** | **Copper Ore**, **Coal Veins**, Limestone | 4x Wood, 2x Flint (Crafting Bench) |
| **Tier 3** | **Copper Pickaxe** | **Iron Ore**, Sulfur Deposits, Hard Granite | 4x Timber, 2x Copper Ingots, 1x Rope |
| **Tier 4** | **Iron Pickaxe** | Deep Iron Veins, Industrial Scrap Blocks | 4x Timber, 2x Iron Ingots, 2x Wire |
| **Tier 5** | **Moonrock Pickaxe** | **Moonrock**, Ancient Lunar Core Fragments | 4x Hardwood, 2x Moonrock Shards, 1x Power Cell |

## 2. Resource Locations & Map Landmarks

### 1. Flint (Surface Boulders)
* **Found:** Scattered along riverbeds, ridge bottoms, and perimeter cliffs of the starting valley.
* **Appearance:** Dark grey stones with a distinct glass-like sheen.

### 2. Copper & Coal (Eastern Mountain Caves)
* **Found:** The rocky canyons east of House Camp and inside subterranean karst tunnels.
* **Appearance:** Copper nodes feature green-tinged veins; Coal nodes are pitch-black jagged rock outcrops.
* **Tip:** Coal yields 3x the burning duration of dry wood in furnaces.

### 3. Iron Ore (Northern Industrial Ridges)
* **Found:** Near abandoned railway tracks, collapsed mineshafts, and the perimeter of the Solar Farm.
* **Appearance:** Rust-red and brownish-black heavy crystalline rocks.

### 4. Moonrock (The Shattering Crater)
* **Found:** Inside the deep impact crater in the far northwestern dead zone.
* **Appearance:** Glowing iridescent cyan-blue minerals emitting mild cosmic radiation. Entering without protective thermal gear triggers severe hypothermia.

## 3. Smelting Ratios in the Stone Furnace

| Input Ore | Fuel Requirement | Output | Smelt Time |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **3x Copper Ore** | 5x Coal (or 8x Wood) | **1x Copper Ingot** | 45 seconds |
| **3x Iron Ore** | 3x Coal | **1x Iron Ingot** | 60 seconds |
| **4x Wood** | Passive heat | **2x Charcoal** | 30 seconds |
| **1x Snow Block** | 1x Kindling | **1x Clean Water Flask** | 15 seconds |
