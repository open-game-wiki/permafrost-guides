When launching a new campaign in *Permafrost*, character creation prompts you to make two distinct tactical choices for your protagonist, **Rook**:
1. **Background:** Determines your starting hotbar inventory and initial gear.
2. **Specialization:** Grants three immediate head-start (+1) skill points in specific attributes.

This guide clarifies how these choices impact early-game survival and which combinations offer the smoothest start.

---

## 1. Starting Backgrounds (Inventory Loadouts)

Your Background defines what supplies you wake up with in the snowdrifts:

| Background | Starting Hotbar Inventory | Playstyle Suitability |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Survivor** *(Recommended)* | • 1x Makeshift Bandage<br>• 1x Improvised Warm Tea<br>• 1x Granola Bar | **Most forgiving start.** The warm tea immediately counters hypothermia if caught in an early blizzard. |
| **Hunter** | • 5x Crude Arrows<br>• 1x Field Skinning Knife<br>• 2x Dried Meat Strips | **Fast food security.** Speeds up animal processing and provides immediate caloric reserves. |
| **Smuggler** | • 1x Bobby Pin Lockpick<br>• 4x Scrap Metal<br>• 1x Pocket Knife | **High-risk looting.** Allows opening locked doors in ruins before crafting locksmith tools. |

## 2. Specializations (Skill Head-Starts)

Every skill in *Permafrost* levels up organically through regular gameplay (e.g., cutting wood levels Axes; running levels Running). Specializations simply provide a **+1 level head start**:

### A. Tracker (Best for Beginners)
* **Skill Bonuses:** `+1 Skinning`, `+1 Bows`, `+1 Knives`
* **Why Choose It:** Hunting with the Crude Bow is the single fastest way to secure food, leather for thermal clothes, and raw meat to tame your dog companion.

### B. Engineer (Best for Base Builders)
* **Skill Bonuses:** `+1 Running`, `+1 Axes`, `+1 Toughness`
* **Why Choose It:** Increases damage dealt to trees, speeds up foot travel, and provides passive damage mitigation from frost and melee hits.

### C. Scavenger (Best for Solo Explorers)
* **Skill Bonuses:** `+1 Lockpicking`, `+1 Sneaking`, `+1 Running`
* **Why Choose It:** Enables silent movement near sleeping wolf packs and facilitates early looting of civilian vehicles and secure lockers.

---

## 3. Recommended Starter Builds

* **The Ultimate Solo Starter:** **Survivor + Tracker**
  * *Why:* You get emergency thermal tea to prevent death in the first 15 minutes, while Tracker bonuses make hunting deer effortless.
* **The Speedrunner Base Builder:** **Survivor + Engineer**
  * *Why:* Extra Toughness gives leeway against cold mistakes, while boosted axe swings accelerate wood gathering for shelter walls.
