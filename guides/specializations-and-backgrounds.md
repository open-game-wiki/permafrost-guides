## The confirmed protagonist

The official description identifies the player character as Rook, an engineer with rebuilding skills and limited wilderness experience. A radio signal from a long-lost friend draws Rook into the journey.

This narrative profession is not evidence of a selectable class system. The official overview describes progression through exploration, shelter restoration, and recovering technology.
